ExpensePRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Our Processing Philosophy

At ExpensePRO, we believe that privacy is not just a legal requirement, but the foundation of conscious financial management. Your interaction with the application is based on three pillars:

  • Data: These are the raw facts and numbers you enter into the system (e.g., an expense amount). They are securely stored with us as a record of your financial reality.
  • Information: This is the data processed by our algorithms. We present it in the form of charts and analyses to help you understand it.
  • Knowledge: This is created exclusively in your mind through the process of interpreting the information received from us. We provide the tools; you build the financial wisdom.

1. Data Collection

We collect only the data necessary for your personal dashboard to function:

  • Account data (username, email, password).
  • Transactional data (amounts, categories, descriptions of expenses and income).

2. Use of Information

Your data is processed solely to generate visual information (charts, statistics) that assists you in the process of interpreting the state of your finances. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Security

We use advanced data protection mechanisms to ensure that the record of your financial reality remains confidential. Every piece of information generated in the system is available only to the logged-in account owner.

4. Your Rights

In accordance with data protection regulations, you have the right to access your data, correct it, and completely delete your account along with all collected financial facts.

If you have any questions regarding your data, please contact us: privacy@expensepro.com
